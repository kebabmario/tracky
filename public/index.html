      About the data

      Sources. SMR 5, 10, 20 — Malaysian Rubber Board daily price (sen/kg). PH rubber — Philippine Statistics Authority national farmgate price for rubber cuplump, monthly and published about 3–4 months late; no public feed carries a daily PSR price. AS and PP block copolymer — prices you capture from SunSirs or 21cp with the Send to Tracky bookmark (CNY/tonne), since those sites block automated access. PVC (SG-5, East China), LDPE (film grade), EVA (foaming grade), PP homo (raffia grade) and ABS — Guidechem GuideTrends daily China spot prices (CNY/tonne), shown per kg; history builds up day by day as Tracky keeps scraping. Copper, aluminum, wire rod — Shanghai Futures Exchange daily settlement price of the most-held contract (CNY/tonne); wire rod futures trade thinly. LME copper and aluminum — London Metal Exchange official cash settlement (US$/tonne), via Westmetall. Metals are shown per tonne.

      Currency. By default each price shows in its market's own currency: SMR in ringgit, PH rubber in pesos, China resin and SHFE metals in yuan, LME metals in US dollars. Picking a currency at the top converts everything with ECB reference rates (via Frankfurter) on each price's own date. The chart uses the group's most common currency until you pick one.
