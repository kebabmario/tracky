# Code of Conduct

## Expectations

This project is maintained in good faith for people who work with commodity prices. Everyone taking
part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent,
- keep feedback specific, technical and about the work rather than the person,
- accept that maintainers make the final call on scope and direction.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, advertising, or repeated off-topic posts.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block participants who
do not follow this code. Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub
(<https://github.com/kebabmario>). Reports are handled confidentially.
